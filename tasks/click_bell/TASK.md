# Task: click_bell

Locate the yellow press cap on the blue tabletop bell, approach it from above, press
once, and retreat to a clear pose. A successful run needs visual evidence that the
cap moved down or the bell body reacted; sound is not required.

Completion requires all of the following:

- the target was localized from a fresh observation;
- exactly one press was attempted;
- the arm retreated after the attempt;
- the gripper was left open;
- the complete task was recorded; and
- the operator supplied a visual result of `success`, `failure`, or `unclear`.

Do not use sound or contact detection as proof of success. A perception-only session
must stop after localization. If localization, planning, execution, or recovery cannot
continue, report the failure without inventing a target or another motion.
